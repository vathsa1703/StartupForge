# 🚀 StartupForge

StartupForge is an AI-powered startup simulator that lets users experience what it's actually like to build a startup.

The idea started with a simple question:

**What if founders could test ideas, talk to customers, pitch investors, and make business decisions before risking real money?**

Instead of reading startup advice, users can create a startup, interact with AI-generated customer personas, pitch AI investors, and make strategic decisions that impact growth, revenue, churn, and runway.

Every choice has consequences, and every simulation plays out differently.

---

## What You Can Do

### 💡 Create a Startup

Start with an idea, describe your product, choose an industry, and generate a market analysis.

### 👥 Talk to AI Customers

Interview customer personas to understand their pain points, buying behavior, and feedback before building.

### 💰 Pitch AI Investors

Present your startup to different investor personalities.

Some care about traction.
Some care about impact.
Some care only about numbers.

Each investor responds differently and remembers your conversations.

### 📈 Run Your Startup

Advance month by month and make decisions across:

* Marketing
* Product
* Team
* Finance

Watch how those decisions affect:

* Monthly recurring revenue (MRR)
* User growth
* Churn
* Burn rate
* Runway

### 📋 Review the Outcome

At the end of a simulation, see how your startup performed and what decisions helped or hurt growth.

## Why I Built This

I've always enjoyed startup stories and entrepreneurship, but most startup learning happens through articles, videos, and case studies.

I wanted to build something interactive.

StartupForge was my attempt to combine AI, simulation, and product thinking into a single experience where users can experiment, fail, learn, and improve like a founder would.

The project also helped me explore:

* Full-stack development
* AI integration
* Prompt engineering
* System design
* Product thinking
